FAQ

Answers to the questions I hear most often from clients and collaborators.

What technologies do you work with?+
I work with a full modern stack including JavaScript (vanilla & jQuery), React, Node.js, Python, and various SQL/NoSQL databases. For frontend, I specialise in crafting responsive, performant UIs with clean animations and pixel-perfect design.
How long does a typical project take?+
Timelines vary by scope. A landing page takes 1–2 weeks, a full web application 6–12 weeks. I provide a detailed project timeline after the initial discovery call, and I use agile sprints to keep delivery predictable and transparent.
